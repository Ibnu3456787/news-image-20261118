**THE WORLD TODAY — Wednesday 18 November 2026.** A one-page newspaper
front page summing up one day of world news: **35 stories, 6 continents,
one day**. Built for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261118`
($250 in USDC).

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
# writes /tmp/out/entry.png (1024x1024, 567,687 bytes, under the 1 MiB limit)
```

Run it twice into fresh directories: the output is byte-identical every
time — the program only copies a fixed file.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) is a complete front page:

- **Masthead** — "THE WORLD TODAY", dated **WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER 2026**,
  with the count line "35 STORIES · 6 CONTINENTS · ONE DAY" and the line
  "Every story below happened, or was first reported, on this day."
- **Lead story** — Iran strikes Kuwait & Bahrain, as a full-width image
  band with a one-line headline and a one-line account beneath it.
- **11 illustrated stories** (numbered 1–11, region-tagged), each with a
  small original flat-editorial illustration drawn for this entry.
- **WHERE IT HAPPENED** — a world map (Natural Earth 110m land polygons)
  with numbered pins at the location of each of the 11 pictured stories.
- **ALSO ON THIS DAY** — a numbered index of stories 12–35.
- **Sources line** — AP, Reuters, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, UN News, NASA,
  WHO, NATO, Nextgov, OpenAI, Google, PNA.

All text is rendered with real fonts (Liberation Serif/Sans). The
illustrations are text-free by design — no text was ever generated by an
image model.

## The 35 stories

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on Wednesday 18
November 2026 (in some timezone), or is that day's development of an
ongoing story, and was verified against the cited source before inclusion.
Seven index items that turned out to be 17 November events were audited
out and replaced with verified 18 November stories (see the audit note in
`research-notes.md`).

## How the image was made

1. **Research** — each of the 35 stories was verified against web news
   sources dated 18 November 2026; unverifiable or misdated items were
   dropped or replaced (see above and `research-notes.md`).
2. **Illustrations** — 24 text-free vignette candidates were generated
   in a consistent flat editorial style (deep navy / warm ochre) for the
   11 illustrated slots; all were reviewed in contact sheets and the
   strongest one per slot was picked. Two were fixed after review: the
   Trump–Marcos flags and the An-12 aircraft type.
3. **Map** — drawn from Natural Earth 110m land polygons (public data),
   equirectangular projection, with numbered pins at the real
   coordinates of the 11 pictured stories; close pins (Gaza/Kuwait,
   Shenzhen/Manila) are nudged apart with leader lines.
4. **Composition** — three full layouts were composed with PIL (Python
   Imaging Library) using real Liberation fonts, compared at full size
   and at 300px, and the strongest was kept: a full-width lead image
   band with a headline band below it, a 4-column tile grid, the map as
   a grid cell, and a two-column index. The composition script is
   `compose.py` in this repo; the layout variants are in `layouts/`.
5. **Entrypoint** — `main.py` (Python standard library only) copies the
   finished `entry.png` to the output directory and verifies it
   byte-for-byte (PNG signature, size limit, sha256).
